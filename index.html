How to post on Instagram

Follow these steps each time you're ready to publish a finished post from the generator.

Step-by-step posting guide
1
Generate & copy your caption
Use the Generate tab to create your caption. Once you're happy with it, click Copy caption. Save it to history so you have a record.
2
Copy your hashtags separately
Click Copy hashtags — keep these separate from the caption. You'll paste them into the first comment after posting, not in the caption itself. This keeps the caption clean.
3
Open Instagram and tap +
Open the Instagram app on your phone. Tap the + icon at the bottom center of the screen to start a new post.
4
Select your photo or video
Choose from your camera roll, or take a new photo. For project photos, select Post (square grid icon). For short videos or process clips, select Reel. Tap Next.
5
Edit & filter (optional)
Apply any filters or adjustments. For Madeleine Interiors, keep edits minimal — warm tones, no heavy filters. Aim to match the natural palette of the space. Tap Next.
6
Paste your caption
Tap the Write a caption… field and paste your copied caption. Read it once more — this is the moment to add one personal sentence if Madeleine wants to. Do not paste the hashtags here.
7
Add location tag
Tap Add location and search for the neighborhood or city — e.g. "Newport Beach, California" or "Brentwood, Los Angeles." Location tagging improves discoverability significantly.
8
Tag collaborators (if relevant)
If the post features a contractor, vendor, or photographer, tap Tag people or use @mention in the caption. For product close-ups, tag the brand (e.g. @greatplainsfabrics, @visualcomfort).
9
Share the post
Tap Share. Your post is now live. Don't close Instagram yet — you have one more step.
10
Paste hashtags in the first comment
Immediately after posting, tap the speech bubble icon on your post to open comments. Tap Add a comment… and paste your copied hashtags. Post the comment. This keeps the caption visually clean while still getting the hashtag reach.
11
Mark as published in history
Come back to the Post history tab and click Mark published on the card. This keeps your records clean and lets you filter what's gone live vs. what's still in the queue.

Quick checklist before every post
Using a scheduler instead
If you prefer to schedule posts in advance, these tools work well with Madeleine Interiors' posting rhythm:
Later — best visual grid planner, drag-and-drop calendar
Planoly — popular with designers, good analytics
Instagram's native scheduler — free, available in the app under Advanced Settings when creating a post. Schedule up to 75 days ahead.
Note: scheduled posts don't auto-add the first-comment hashtags — you'll still need to do that manually after the post goes live.
